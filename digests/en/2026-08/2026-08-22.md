### ⭐ [Fix GIN posting tree page deletion with incomplete splits.](https://github.com/postgres/postgres/commit/f20c4278342f6afc44b856e98a0850f9dd2df0a8)
**Author: Peter Geoghegan**

This commit fixes a critical bug where GIN posting tree page deletion could incorrectly handle incompletely split pages. This could lead to a disconnected sibling chain and potential data corruption during GIN index maintenance.

---

### ⭐ [Track RI fast-path FK-check batches per subtransaction](https://github.com/postgres/postgres/commit/e2c812f1475dc75ab6f8a39fb5696d8d32d05fa1)
**Author: Amit Langote**

This fix re-enables the RI fast-path batching optimization for foreign key checks within subtransactions, such as those used during `COPY` into tables with FKs inside a `SAVEPOINT`. This restores significant performance that was lost when batching was previously confined only to the top transaction level.

---

### [Retire execdebug.h and its debugging facility](https://github.com/postgres/postgres/commit/617636328f5285fa2e0b7e42f4ae3cb0d82f8b5e)
**Author: Michael Paquier**

The `execdebug.h` header and its associated executor debugging macros have been removed from the codebase. This "pretty old and crufty" facility was non-compiling for over a decade and has been superseded by better alternatives like `elog()`.
