### ⭐ [Support UUIDv6 in uuid_extract_timestamp().](https://github.com/postgres/postgres/commit/451871b740d7ad252b8aeaf16a20bcf831a39254)
**Masahiko Sawada**

This commit adds support for extracting timestamps from UUIDv6 values within the `uuid_extract_timestamp()` function. Previously, this function only supported UUIDv1 and UUIDv7.

**Why it matters:** As UUIDv6 gains traction, supporting its timestamp extraction allows PostgreSQL users to leverage this information directly, enabling better indexing, partitioning, and analysis of data based on this newer UUID standard.

### ⭐ [Checkpoint replication slots later in the checkpoint cycle](https://github.com/postgres/postgres/commit/69ed7fd7e9da1cff2f04af04f630287971fe99fe)
**Fujii Masao**

This change reorders the checkpointing of replication slots to occur later in the checkpoint cycle, after buffer writes and sync requests are processed. This reduces the time window where replication slots might hold onto older WAL segments unnecessarily.

**Why it matters:** By ensuring `replicationSlotMinLSN` is computed from the most up-to-date information, this optimization can lead to earlier cleanup of WAL segments, potentially reducing disk usage and improving efficiency, especially during spread checkpoints.
