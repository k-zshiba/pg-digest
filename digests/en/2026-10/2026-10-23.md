### ⭐ [Emit null-keyed hash join outer tuples immediately when feasible.](https://github.com/postgres/postgres/commit/126976a8ffcfa47f40d3df09243ebf4606001912)
**Author: Tom Lane**

This commit modifies hash join behavior to immediately emit outer tuples with null join keys in non-parallel and single-batch cases, avoiding potential expensive spills to disk.

**Why it matters:** This change prevents a performance regression from v18 by optimizing how null-keyed tuples are handled, ensuring they are processed efficiently without unnecessary disk I/O in common scenarios.
