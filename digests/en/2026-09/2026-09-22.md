### ⭐ [Avoid replay cleanup locks for freeze-only and VM-only records](https://github.com/postgres/postgres/commit/07c73f45063f6fcdc7f460aec84396ae4e0f71c5)
**Melanie Plageman**

Avoids unconditionally requesting cleanup locks during WAL replay for freeze-only and visibility map (VM)-only records. A cleanup lock is now requested in redo only when the record actually performs pruning, reducing lock contention during recovery.

---

### [Assert correct VM page passed to pruning](https://github.com/postgres/postgres/commit/d8516db39dfa73042e3ba28f9efc78da4dd6792d)
**Melanie Plageman**

Adds an assertion to verify that the correct visibility map page is pinned before pruning a heap page. This prevents pin leaks and stale references during development.

### [Convert SharedFileSet->refcnt to an atomic variable.](https://github.com/postgres/postgres/commit/09a579abaca86bcd85ebc49b247d2ec58bdc6d3b)
**Nathan Bossart** — *Discussion: [alAJeRRzehDjLaF1@nathan](https://postgr.es/m/alAJeRRzehDjLaF1%40nathan)*

Converts the reference counter of shared file sets (`SharedFileSet->refcnt`) from an integer protected by a spinlock to an atomic variable, eliminating the spinlock overhead.

### [Convert Sharedsort's worker counters to atomic variables.](https://github.com/postgres/postgres/commit/ee97eef695e8be9153595131f8b63e4b1b3e8196)
**Nathan Bossart**

Converts worker-related counters in parallel sort (`Sharedsort`) to atomic variables. This removes spinlock overhead and improves synchronization efficiency among parallel sort worker processes.

### [Convert PROC_HDR->startupBufferPinWaitBuf to an atomic variable.](https://github.com/postgres/postgres/commit/cb4a03db9ed7606da1c36e4214a6c5fd6437a26c)

### ⭐ [pg_upgrade: Check for arrays over system types with auto-assigned OIDs](https://github.com/postgres/postgres/commit/77f54eb07488341025073a370f6e381a6ee05e04)
**John Naylor**

Adds a check in `pg_upgrade` for arrays stored over system types (e.g., `information_schema` domains) that have auto-assigned OIDs. This prevents database corruption and read/dump failures due to dangling OIDs after a major version upgrade.
