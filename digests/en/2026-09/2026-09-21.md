### Simplify abbreviated key hashing during cardinality estimation
**John Naylor**

This commit simplifies abbreviated key hashing during cardinality estimation by leveraging 64-bit Datums, removing the need for 32-bit folding. It introduces a direct murmurhash64() call, streamlining the process and avoiding an extra function call, also replacing hash_uint32() with murmurhash32() in related areas.
