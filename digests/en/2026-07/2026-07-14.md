# 2026-07-14 PostgreSQL News Digest

## Commits

Today's commits largely focused on refining the recently enhanced JSON_TABLE functionality, addressing several bugs and improving its behavior. There was also a significant internal change to improve C standard compliance across different compilers.

### ⭐ [Replace __builtin_types_compatible_p with _Generic](https://github.com/postgres/postgres/commit/d15a6bc2e16d4b330d6d455e41ce1ab3395d8e03)
**Peter Eisentraut**

This commit replaces the GCC-specific `__builtin_types_compatible_p` macro with the C11 standard-compliant `_Generic` keyword. This change impacts internal static assertion macros and utility functions like `unconstify()` and `unvolatize()`.

**Why it matters:** This is an important step towards improving PostgreSQL's portability and standard compliance, ensuring its codebase works reliably with a wider range of C compilers beyond just GCC-compatible ones.

---

### [Avoid redundant re-evaluation of JSON_TABLE nested paths](https://github.com/postgres/postgres/commit/6d8fd5a88183a65e77308aff374ebc239c44c6af)
**Alexander Korotkov**

This commit fixes a performance regression where JSON_TABLE with a NESTED PATH re-evaluated the nested path's jsonpath expression multiple times per parent row, significantly slowing down queries with growing nested arrays. The issue stemmed from leftover logic from the plan/join executor rework.

---

### [Revert cascading of JSON_TABLE's ON ERROR](https://github.com/postgres/postgres/commit/af6fad879fbf9b542cfde05c52695c9dffa6bb47)
**Alexander Korotkov**

The behavior of JSON_TABLE's ON ERROR clause was reverted; it no longer implicitly cascades a table-level ON ERROR to columns lacking their own. While the SQL standard mandates this, the change was deemed too significant for a silent introduction and will be re-evaluated as a deliberate, documented feature.

---

### [Make JSON_TABLE generated path names avoid collisions](https://github.com/postgres/postgres/commit/cd5fd08be7ec1cef1a4c34791ce743954c2b2293)
**Alexander Korotkov**

This commit addresses a bug where automatically generated path names for unnamed NESTED paths in JSON_TABLE could collide with user-supplied names. Such collisions previously led to silent dropping of columns from the output.

---

### [Fix JSON_TABLE PLAN deparse to keep parentheses around nested joins](https://github.com/postgres/postgres/commit/160ef2751b524cbc797e59642543790c4a8132ec)
**Alexander Korotkov**

A parsing bug was fixed in JSON_TABLE's PLAN clause deparsing, ensuring that parentheses are correctly retained around nested parent/child joins. Previously, this omission could lead to incorrect re-parsing of plans, creating a potential dump/restore hazard.

---

### [Fix and polish JSON_TABLE documentation](https://github.com/postgres/postgres/commit/4a82912bb2a2bc757142eca48f0fded1817a9a7c)
**Alexander Korotkov**

This commit provides various documentation improvements for JSON_TABLE, including correcting the synopsis order for the ON ERROR clause, updating terminology, and ensuring consistent capitalization and ordering of keywords like OUTER/INNER.

---

### [Remove unreachable error check in JSON_TABLE plan transform](https://github.com/postgres/postgres/commit/9ee5241d02442911ffec9a0ce35327a0fea4a5c1)
**Alexander Korotkov**

An unreachable error check within the `transformJsonTableNestedColumns()` function was removed. The validation logic had already been moved to an earlier stage, making the subsequent check redundant and impossible to trigger.
